**Snipping Tool for video.** Press the shortcut, click a window, panel or the frame inside it (or drag a box), record with sound, and the MP4 lands in your Videos folder and on the clipboard.

### Downloads
- **Windows 10/11:** `Snipzo-Portable-*.exe`, one file, no install (lives in the tray). Shortcut: Win + Shift + R.
- **Mac, Apple silicon (M1 and later):** `Snipzo-*-mac-arm64.dmg`
- **Mac, Intel:** `Snipzo-*-mac-x64.dmg`

Mac: not signed by Apple, so the first time right-click Snipzo › Open › Open. Allow it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording when asked, then quit and reopen it. Shortcut: Option + Shift + R. No system sound on Mac (macOS doesn't share it); the mic works. Start on Space is Windows-only for now.

### New in 0.3.0
- macOS builds (Apple silicon and Intel).
- Edit a selection before recording: drag edges or corners, drag inside to move, arrow keys to nudge, Ctrl+Z to undo.
- Start on Space (Windows): a snip that begins when you tap Space in your app (After Effects preview) and ends at the next tap, cut exactly.
- After Effects comp frames snap to the exact pixel, even when the comp fills the viewer's height.
- The shortcut keeps working after cancelling with Esc (the keyboard hook now runs on its own thread).

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksum of every file.
